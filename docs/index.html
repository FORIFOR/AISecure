MEASURED / NOT CLAIMED

We publish what it
fails to detect.

We could not find another tool in this space that publishes a detection rate. We measured ours against our own corpus and put the weak parts first.

What this number does not mean

It is measured on a synthetic 60-case corpus (40 positive, 20 negative). It is not a miss rate on real business documents, and deliberate evasion such as a zero-width character is out of scope.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Three boundaries worth knowing.

Does installing it protect the whole endpoint or VPN?

No. The managed send path must be integrated. Endpoint DLP and production VPN containment are separate work.

Does a generative model decide what is safe?

The default decision is rule-based. An optional local model can explain facts, but it has no decision or execution authority.

Can I upload real logs to this public site?

No. This public site is for product presentation and synthetic examples. Handle authorized real logs locally.
